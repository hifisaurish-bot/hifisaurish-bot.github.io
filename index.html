Stage 1

JEE Main (Conducted by NTA)

Official Portal: jeemain.nta.nic.in →

Paper 1: B.E. / B.Tech

  • Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (100 marks each).
  • Questions: 75 total (25 per subject = 20 MCQs in Section A + 5 compulsory numericals in Section B).
  • Marking: +4 correct, −1 wrong (both Section A & B), 0 unattempted.
  • Duration & Mode: 3 hours, Computer-Based Test (CBT) in 13 languages.

Paper 2A & 2B: B.Arch / B.Plan

  • Paper 2A (B.Arch): 400 marks total — Mathematics (100), Aptitude (200), and offline Drawing Test (100 on drawing sheet).
  • Paper 2B (B.Planning): 400 marks total — Mathematics, Aptitude, and Planning-based questions in CBT mode.
  • Duration: 3 hours per paper (no calculators or electronic gadgets allowed).

Sessions & Eligibility

  • Two Sessions: Session 1 (January) and Session 2 (April). Appearing in both is optional; the best NTA score counts.
  • Age Limit: No age limit to appear in JEE Main.
  • Gateway To: Admission to NITs, IIITs, CFTIs/GFTIs, and qualification for JEE Advanced.

Stage 2

JEE Advanced (For IIT Admissions)

Official Portal: jeeadv.ac.in →

Eligibility & Attempt Rules

  • JEE Main Rank: Must be among the top 2,50,000 candidates (all categories) in JEE Main Paper 1.
  • Attempt Limit: Maximum 2 attempts in 2 consecutive years (your Class 12 year and the year immediately after).
  • Prior IIT Admission: Candidates who previously accepted an IIT seat are not eligible.
  • Class 12 Board Criteria: Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics mandatory; 75% aggregate (or top 20 percentile of the board) applies for IIT admission after qualifying.

Exam Structure & Question Types

  • Conducting IITs: Rotates among 7 IITs (IIT Roorkee in 2026; IIT Delhi in 2027).
  • Two Compulsory Papers: Paper 1 and Paper 2, 3 hours each on the same day (CBT in English & Hindi). Skipping either disqualifies you.
  • Dynamic Marking: Total marks and marking scheme vary each year and appear in on-screen instructions (e.g., 360 total marks in 2026: 180 per paper).
  • Question Formats: Single-correct MCQs, Multiple-Select (MSQ) with partial/negative marks, Numerical Value, and Match/Paragraph questions.
  • AAT (B.Arch at IITs): Candidates seeking B.Arch at IIT (BHU) Varanasi, IIT Kharagpur, or IIT Roorkee must also pass the 3-hour Architecture Aptitude Test after qualifying JEE Advanced.
